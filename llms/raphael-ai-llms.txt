# Raphael AI

> Raphael AI (https://raphael.app) is a free, unlimited AI image and video
> generator. No sign-up or credit card required — describe what you want in
> plain language (32 languages supported) and get high-quality results in
> seconds. The only official website is https://raphael.app.

Raphael AI turns text prompts into high-fidelity images and videos using top
models (Nano Banana, Seedream, GPT Image, Veo, Kling and more), all in one
place. Built for everyone — no prompt-engineering skills needed. Free users get
genuinely unlimited standard-quality generation; paid plans add higher
resolution, faster speed, and exclusive features.

## Core Tools
- [AI Image Generator (Text to Image)](https://raphael.app/text-to-image): Turn a text description into images.
- [AI Image Editor](https://raphael.app/ai-image-editor): Edit and transform existing photos with AI.
- [Image to Image](https://raphael.app/image-to-image): Reimagine an uploaded image from a prompt.
- [Uncrop / Image Extend](https://raphael.app/uncrop): Extend image borders naturally (outpainting).
- [Background Remover](https://raphael.app/background-remover): Remove image backgrounds in one click.
- [AI Video Generator](https://raphael.app/ai-video-generator): Generate videos from text or images.
- [Image to Video](https://raphael.app/image-to-video): Animate a still image into video.
